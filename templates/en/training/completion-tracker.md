## Completion records

ISO/IEC 5230 §3.1.2.3 and 18974 §4.1.2.4 require evidence of **assessed competence**.
A record saying "attended the training" is not enough on its own; you need evidence that the completion criteria were met.
Example **completion criteria**: a quiz score of 80 or above (developers), a signed confirmation (managers), confirmation that the hands-on exercise was finished (operations).
